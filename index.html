Support

Ball Duel · real-time 1v1 ball battles

Need help, found a bug, or have feedback? Email us at playballduel@gmail.com and include your player name (shown in Settings).

Common questions

A purchase didn't arrive. Make sure you're online and restart the game. If it's still missing, email us your player name and the purchase date. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I change my name? Settings → Name → CHANGE. You can change it once.

How do I report or block a player? Hold their name on the leaderboard or your friends list.

How do I delete my account? Settings → DELETE ACCOUNT. This can't be undone.
