Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

This policy explains what information Ball Duel ("the game", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Ball Duel is made by Aaron Yang, an independent developer based in New York, USA. Questions: playballduel@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account and game data. When you first open the game, an anonymous player account is created for you (through Unity Gaming Services). We store your player ID, your player name, your game progress (trophies, balls, faces, emotes, coins, gems, rewards and settings), your friends list and friend requests, players you have blocked, and your leaderboard entry.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. We receive a record of what you bought so we can deliver it to your account.
  • Reports. If you report another player's name, we store the report (the reported player, the name, and your player ID) so we can review it.
  • Usage and diagnostics. We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played (for example which screens are used, matches played, and purchases made), Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports, and Firebase Remote Config to adjust game settings. These collect device and app information such as device model, operating system, app version, an app-instance identifier, and an approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address.
  • Advertising. The game offers optional rewarded ads (for example to double coins) through Unity LevelPlay and its ad partners. Ads are non-personalized: we do not ask for permission to track you across apps, we do not access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), and we do not use your data for targeted advertising. Ad partners may still collect technical information needed to serve and measure an ad (such as device type, IP address and ad interactions).
  • Videos you choose to save. If you use SHARE CLIP, the game makes a video of your match on your device. It is only saved to your Photos or shared if you choose to, and we do not receive a copy.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and we do not sell your personal information.

How we use it

  • To run the game: saving your progress, matchmaking, friends, challenges, leaderboards and delivering purchases.
  • To keep the game fair and safe, including reviewing name reports and enforcing our Terms of Use.
  • To fix bugs and crashes and improve the game.
  • To show optional rewarded ads.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the game, under their own privacy terms:

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services: accounts, cloud save, multiplayer, friends, leaderboards; and Unity LevelPlay / Unity Ads)
  • Google (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config)
  • Apple (App Store and in-app purchases)
  • Ad networks used through Unity LevelPlay, for rewarded ads only

Other players can see your player name, league, trophies and leaderboard position, and your online status if you are friends. We may also disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and game data for as long as your account exists. Analytics and crash data are kept for limited periods set by our providers. When you delete your account, your account data is deleted as described below.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the game: open Settings and tap DELETE ACCOUNT. This permanently deletes your account, game progress, friends, leaderboard entry and player name. Purchases cannot be restored after deletion. You can also email us at playballduel@gmail.com to request deletion.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to certain uses. To make a request, email playballduel@gmail.com. We do not sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Children

Ball Duel is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We rely on the security measures of our providers to protect your data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above, and for significant changes we will let you know in the game.
